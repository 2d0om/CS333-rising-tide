Overview · choose a time window

Drag the box or its edges, or use the period buttons. Drag the ▼ to jump to any month of the playback. Strip = mortgage registrations per 100 sales (100% stacked, 3-month mean). Top-right: the window’s value, or the hovered month’s. Ribbon = market phase.

Encoding (marks and channels)
  • Detail — connected scatterplot: one dot per month; x = CBUAE policy rate (%), y = monthly sales in the sample. Segments join consecutive months, so the line is the market’s path through time.
  • Line width = mortgage registrations per 100 sales that month (3-month mean). Colour = market phase (five periods defined by the rate path).
  • Dashed grey arrow = schematic textbook expectation (higher rates → fewer sales); faint until you hover it.
  • The Governor’s dial = the policy rate of the month in focus (needle angle, 0–6%). Sheet of 100 notes = how many of every 100 sales had a matching mortgage registration (coloured notes). Tape = the last three months in focus: rate, sales and direction of change.
  • Overview — 100% stacked area: x = month; bottom band (teal) = mortgage registrations per sale, top band (grey) = “no matching mortgage registration”. Brush = selected window; ribbon = market phase.
Interaction guide
  • Brush the overview or use the team’s period buttons (2015–17, 2018–20, 2021–23, 2024–26); releasing snaps to whole months with an elastic ease (d3.easeBackOut).
  • Play / Pause / Reset / − +: replays the window month by month (1× = 0.4 s per month; − and + change the speed). The dial needle swings with spring overshoot, and the tape and notes update.
  • Jump to a month: click any point on the chart, or drag the ▼ marker above the overview. Play then continues from there.
  • Hover the chart: flashlight lines project the month to both axes and mark it on the overview; dial, notes, tape and the overview corner all show that month.
  • Linking: the window is broadcast on BroadcastChannel('rising-tide') as {from,to} month indexes (0 = Jan 2015). Final linking method still to be agreed with the team.
