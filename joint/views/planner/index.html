Where they are

Data. Dubai Land Department transactions, 10% random sample (course file): sales of units with sub-type Flat, 1 Jan 2015 – 31 Aug 2026 (older records are sparse and partly implausible; September 2026 is incomplete), dates read as UTC. Shares, not counts, because the file is a sample. Residents: Dubai Data and Statistics Establishment, estimated population by community (usual residents, 2018–2025), joined on the community code. Each period uses the estimate nearest its middle: 2015–17 uses 2018 (the earliest available), 2018–20 uses 2019, 2021–23 uses 2022 and 2024–26 uses 2025. Its 2019 figures match the course file's Population 2019. Metro: the 56 stations in the course repo (Red and Green lines and the Expo branch, as of about 2020); distance is a straight line from the community's centre to the nearest station, so large communities may have a station nearer their edge. "The busiest" = the fewest communities that together make half of all flat sales in the period. Ready or off-plan: ready flats (registered as existing) can be lived in from the sale; off-plan flats were sold before completion, so people move in after handover; the file has no completion dates, and some buyers are investors who will rent the flat out. Flat size: from the bedrooms recorded on each sale (rooms_en); used as a sign of household type, not a record of who the buyers are (studio or 1-bed, 2-bed, 3-bed or more); "% = 2-bed+" is the share of flats with two or more bedrooms; sales with no bedroom count are left out of that bar.
